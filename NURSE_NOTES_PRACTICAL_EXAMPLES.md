## Scenario 2: Respiratory Infection (Rejection)

### Patient Submits:
```
"I have been coughing for 3 days. No fever. Just dry cough."
```

### AI Assessment Returns:
```
"Symptoms are mild and consistent with common cold or upper respiratory infection.
Recommend rest, fluids, and over-the-counter remedies."
```

### Nurse Reviews & Rejects:
The nurse evaluates and decides:
- Symptoms are too mild for specialist care
- AI assessment is appropriate
- Patient should see primary care if persists

Nurse clicks REJECT and adds reason:

```
"This case does not require specialist consultation at this time.

REASON FOR REJECTION:
Your symptoms are consistent with a common cold or mild upper respiratory 
infection. The recommended care (rest, fluids, OTC medications) is appropriate.

WHAT YOU SHOULD DO:
1) Follow the AI recommendations: rest and hydration
2) Take over-the-counter pain relievers for symptoms
3) Use honey or cough drops for cough relief
4) Call your primary care physician if symptoms persist beyond 7 days
5) Seek immediate care if you develop: high fever, difficulty breathing, 
   or chest pain

NO SPECIALIST APPOINTMENT NEEDED at this time.

If your symptoms worsen or don't improve, feel free to resubmit your case 
for another evaluation."

## Scenario 4: Specialist Follow-up (Post-Surgery)

### Patient Submits:
```
"It's been 3 weeks since knee surgery. The surgical site is healing well, 
but I still have moderate pain when standing for more than 10 minutes. 
I'm on physical therapy but want to know if this is normal."
```

### AI Assessment Returns:
```
"Post-surgical pain at 3 weeks is common. Physical therapy is appropriate.
Recommend orthopedic follow-up to ensure proper healing."
```

### Nurse Reviews & Approves:

```
"Approved for POST-SURGICAL FOLLOW-UP EVALUATION

Your recovery seems to be progressing normally!

APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING:
- Type: Post-operative follow-up
- Specialist: Dr. Johnson (Orthopedic Surgery)
- Duration: 20-30 minutes
- Timing: Can be done in next 1-2 weeks

WHAT TO BRING:
1) Insurance card and government ID
2) List of current medications (especially pain relievers)
3) Any medical records from your surgery
4) List of any concerns since surgery

BEFORE YOUR VISIT:
- Continue physical therapy as prescribed
- Keep track of: pain levels, swelling, movement improvements
- Note any unusual symptoms (increased swelling, redness, warmth, drainage)
- Wear comfortable clothes that allow easy access to surgical site

DURING VISIT:
- Doctor will examine the surgical site
- Check range of motion and healing progress
- Discuss pain management
- May adjust physical therapy recommendations
- Take any discharge instructions seriously

PAIN MANAGEMENT:
- Continue prescribed pain medications as directed
- Use ice (15 minutes at a time) as needed
- Elevate leg when resting
- Report increased pain or swelling immediately

RED FLAGS - Contact us immediately if you experience:
- Increased swelling or redness
- Warmth at surgical site
- Pus or unusual drainage
- Fever (over 101.5°F)
- Severe pain not controlled by medication
- Chest pain or difficulty breathing

Keep up the good work with physical therapy!"

## 🎯 Common Nurse Note Elements

Nurses typically include in their approval notes:

### 1. **Documents to Bring**
```
Please bring:
- Government-issued ID
- Insurance card
- Medical records
- Medication list
- Previous imaging/test results
- Pain diary/symptom log
```

### 2. **Pre-Appointment Instructions**
```
Before your appointment:
- Avoid food/drink (if required)
- Stop certain medications (if applicable)
- Avoid strenuous activity
- Wear comfortable clothing
- Arrive 15 minutes early
- Bring a list of questions
```

### 3. **Specialist Information**
```
- Doctor name and specialty
- Clinic name and address
- Phone number and hours
- Expected duration
- Parking information
- Payment/insurance details
```

### 4. **What to Expect**
```
During your appointment:
- Initial check-in and vitals
- Medical history discussion
- Physical examination
- Possible testing (imaging, blood work)
- Discussion of treatment options
- Timeline for next steps
```

### 5. **Post-Appointment Care**
```
After your appointment:
- Follow medication schedule strictly
- Complete prescribed physical therapy
- Watch for warning signs
- Schedule follow-up appointment
- Keep detailed health diary
- Contact with any questions
```

### 6. **Warning Signs**
```
Seek immediate care if you experience:
- Severe pain
- High fever
- Difficulty breathing
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe bleeding
- Any emergency symptoms
```
